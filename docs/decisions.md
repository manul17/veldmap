# Design Decisions

Concise record of **implemented** architectural decisions—focusing on *why alternatives were rejected* and *hard cross-slice invariants* that cannot be inferred from reading the code alone.

> **Policy:** Record a decision here only once it is implemented and proven in code (or when preserving a hard numerical/safety invariant). Unbuilt future slices live as working hypotheses in [plan.md](../plan.md), not pre-accepted contracts. Module implementation details live in Rust `//!` docstrings (`cargo doc`).

---

## 1. Native Rust Workspace & UI/GPU-Free Domain Core

- **Why not WASI / Wasmtime / protobuf IPC:** VeldMap's legacy prototype routed components through a WASI/Wasmtime event bus with protobuf serialization, adding IPC overhead, `!Send` constraints, and 32-bit guest memory limits with no desktop benefit. `wasmtime`, `prost`, and `tonic` are banned in [deny.toml](../deny.toml) to prevent regressions.
- **Why keep `veldmap-core` free of UI/GPU crates:** Keeping `egui`, `eframe`, and `wgpu` out of `veldmap-core` ensures fast headless builds/tests without graphics drivers or windowing libraries and preserves reuse in future companions.
- **Workspace crate growth:** Split workspace crates along real dependency seams (`veldmap-core` pure leaf, `veldmap-raster` landed in Slice 2A, `veldmap-storage` and `veldmap-net` landed in Slice 2B) rather than simulating crate boundaries via feature flags.

## 2. Dependency Hygiene & Safety Defaults

- **No `memmap2` for user files (banned in [deny.toml](../deny.toml)):** Memory-mapped I/O in Rust risks `SIGBUS` UB on truncated, removable, or network-backed files. Positioned I/O (`pread`/`seek_read`) with caller buffers is safe, portable, and mirrors HTTP Range reads. (Wayland SHM wrappers `smithay-client-toolkit` and `winit` remain exempted.)
- **TLS via `rustls` + `ring`, no OpenSSL or heavy ORMs:** `openssl`/`openssl-sys` and `sqlx` are banned in [deny.toml](../deny.toml) to avoid cross-OS TLS linking issues and heavyweight ORM plumbing. `veldmap-net` explicitly configures `reqwest` with `rustls-no-provider` and `rustls`'s `ring` provider (compiled via `cc` with zero external CMake/NASM build tools while satisfying `deny.toml` SPDX checks).
- **No external C geospatial libraries (`gdal`/`proj`); self-contained bundled SQLite & ZSTD:** Default builds avoid external C system libraries (`gdal`, `proj`, `libhdf5`) and keep `veldmap-core` permanently pure-Rust, while self-contained `cc`-compiled crates (`ring` for TLS, `rusqlite` `bundled` for the ephemeral tile cache §6, and `zstd-sys` via `tiff`'s `zstd` feature §7) remain allowed when justified.

## 3. Smooth WGS84 Ellipsoid (`height = 0.0`), Reverse-Z & 2 m Floor

- **Why no 3D DEM mesh displacement in v1:** Orthorectified optical satellite imagery and cm-scale drone orthophotos place pixels at true geodetic `(lat, lon)`. Draping imagery onto a mismatched DEM (e.g., 30 m COP-DEM over a cm-scale drone orthophoto built on its own DSM) shifts pixels horizontally by `Δh × tan(θ)` (orders of magnitude above `< 2 mm`), introduces view-dependent relief parallax, conflicts with zero-resampling native-CRS warp meshes, and requires terrain-ray intersections, elevated shadow-cone horizon culling, depth-tested vector draping, and EGM2008 vertical-datum conversion. A flat, map-consistent ellipsoid is therefore the right **analytical default** (swipe, diff, readout, 2D/3D sync); terrain relief will be delivered as quantitative GPU layers (hillshade, slope, aspect; Slice 5B-1), and any future v2 3D terrain relief belongs in a separate view mode rather than replacing the flat surface.
- **Current nadir camera:** VeldMap v1 uses a nadir camera (`Camera`) for uniform center GSD and simple controls; zero camera tilt is a v1 UX simplification (not physically required to avoid relief parallax on a flat `height = 0.0` surface). Because v1 `Camera`, `cull::CullFrustum`, `geodesy::surface_at`, and depth-less draped primitives optimize for `height_m = 0.0`, any future v2 3D terrain or oblique mode will extend or replace those modules rather than layering transparently via synchronous height-oracle traits.
- **Why `MIN_HEIGHT_M = 2.0` m with `f64` infinite Reverse-Z & depth-less overlays:** The legacy renderer floated draped lines on an 80 m `SURFACE_LIFT_M` to clear coarse mesh chord sag, forcing an 800 m camera floor (~0.75 m/px at 1000 rows, 50° FOV—37× too coarse for 2 cm drone orthophotos). Moreover, independently triangulated smooth-ellipsoid meshes (e.g., a coarse native-CRS warp mesh over a finer basemap quadtree) have different chord sagittas and clip or Z-fight if depth-tested against each other. Rendering the draped graticule and overlay passes back-to-front **depth-less** (`depth_write: false`, `depth_compare: Always` or `depth_stencil_attachment: None` post-body passes) with fragment horizon discard removes `SURFACE_LIFT_M`, lowers the floor to `2.0` m (~1.87 mm/px at 1000 rows, 50° FOV), and keeps `Depth32Float` (`RENDER_ATTACHMENT | TRANSIENT_ATTACHMENT` + `StoreOp::Discard`) memoryless on TBDR GPUs without `COPY_SRC` depth readback.
- **Exact 1:1 sRGB compositing:** Offscreen rendering targets `Rgba8UnormSrgb` with `view_formats: &[Rgba8Unorm]` and linear RGB constants, compositing 1:1 via `FilterMode::Nearest` using the view matching the swapchain color space (zero manual `encode_srgb` hacks).

## 4. Overlay Accuracy & Readout Contract (Cross-Slice Benchmark)

Raster warp-mesh placement (`a_raster_lands_where_its_binding_puts_it`, enforced since Slice 3A-1) verifies surface placement (including `f32` vertex encoding) across all 5 benchmark bindings in UTM zone 37 (`EPSG:32637`):

1. Drone orthophoto at **2 cm/px**
2. Sentinel-2 granule at **10 m/px**
3. Landsat scene at **30 m/px**
4. Sentinel-2 granule at **pyramid level 4**
5. **8000×5000** orthophoto **rotated by 30°**

Tolerances (Slice 3A-1 benchmark):
- `< 10⁻³` of the pixel size of the drawn pyramid level
- `< 2 mm` for any raster drawn at its native resolution
- `< 2 cm` at any UTM-scale pyramid level (coarse regional/global cells wider than `~8°` cap subdivision at `0.5°/segment`, staying strictly `< 10⁻³` px)
- Paired with additional CRS fixtures (`EPSG:28407`, `EPSG:32737`, `EPSG:3857`) and an end-to-end **`PixelIsArea` vs. `PixelIsPoint` (`GTRasterTypeGeoKey`)** half-pixel check.

Cross-slice numerical & readout invariants:
- **CPU placement + close-up GPU verification:** Assert `< 10⁻³` px placement on CPU exact geometry and texture coordinates (GPU bilinear filtering is typically 8-bit / `1/256` texel), paired with close-up (`2 m`) between-vertices GPU smoke tests verifying cell-midpoint sag and LOD continuity.
- **Display sampling ≠ scientific readout:** Cursor clicks identify the visible geographic location; scientific cursor readouts and time-series queries must read native source datasets on CPU with explicit resolution, interpolation, and nodata rules so values remain zoom-invariant as sharper display tiles stream in.

## 5. `f64` ECEF Metres, Geodetic Nadir Aim & Decoupled Quadtree Geometry (Slice 2A)

- **Why `f64` ECEF metres + per-tile CPU `f64` RTC (instead of normalized units and GPU double-single RTE):** Expressing ECEF directly in metres unifies `geodesy::Ellipsoid` (Fukushima 2006 3D inverse) with `projection::Datum` Helmert shifts and eliminates scale-factor conversions across `Camera`, `cull::CullFrustum`, and tile geometry. Computing `(tile_origin - eye)` on CPU in `f64` and storing tile vertices relative to `tile_origin` in `f32` preserves ~24 µm precision at `2 m` altitude without relying on non-reassociating GPU float math across Vulkan/DX12/Metal compilers.
- **Why geodetic nadir aim (`forward = -self.at`):** Aiming geocentrically toward Earth's center introduces a `~0.192°` mid-latitude offset (~3.6 px at 1000 rows) and `~14.7 km` orbit-to-2 m center-zoom drift. Aiming along `-self.at` makes `Camera::probe(0.5, 0.5, aspect)` agree identically with `Camera::center()` across all altitudes and latitudes.
- **Why decoupled geometry vs. texture LOD (`z_geom <= 16–17`) + adaptive Polar Stereographic caps:** Basemaps cap out at `max_level`, whereas `2 m` viewing requires sub-millimetre chord sag. Subdividing geometry to `z_geom = 16–17` while clamping texture requests to `<= max_tex_level` and sampling ancestors guarantees `0` requests above `max_tex_level` and `< 1 mm` sag at `2 m`. Covering `|lat| >= 85.05112878°` with elliptical square-to-disk Polar Stereographic quadtree caps eliminates polar slivers and antimeridian UV seams while stitching seamlessly via sag-scaled outward-flared skirts and 2:1 neighbor balancing.
- **Why post-overlay procedural `fwidth()` graticule & `GpuTile` hygiene:** Evaluating the graticule procedurally in a transparent post-overlay pass (with uniform-flow `fwidth()` strictly before horizon `discard`) removes chord parallax and prevents raster overlays from burying it. Keeping `GpuTile` strictly limited to immutable texture/view/format/dimensions (`valid_w`, `valid_h`, `gutter`)/byte-size/ID—with `TileCache` delegating ancestor walks to `TileScheme::parent` (exposing UV-decoupled `find_carrier` for clipped/guttered `RasterPyramid` tiles) and `GlobeRenderer` sweeping `(Weak<GpuTile>, wgpu::BindGroup)` entries on `prepare()`—lets one cache contract serve planetary basemaps and local/cloud raster pyramids without inverting parent direction or leaking GPU textures across discarded `egui` passes.

## 6. Async Tile Streaming, CPU Linear-Light Mipmaps, Vertically Partitioned SQLite WAL Cache & Live Basemaps (Slice 2B)

- **Why zero-dep async-waking `CancelToken` and `Wakeup` in `veldmap-core::sync`:** Keeping `CancelToken`, `Wakeup`, and `lock_unpoisoned` in `veldmap-core::sync` (with `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` across `veldmap-core`) lets synchronous raster decoders, disk-cache workers, local `ByteSource`s, and `tokio::select!` HTTP fetches share cancellation and repaint-wakeup primitives without leaking `tokio` into `veldmap-core` or `veldmap-net` into storage/bundled-basemap workers.
- **Why CPU linear-light mipmaps + `TwoLaneDecodePool` / `DecodePool` in `veldmap-raster` (decoupled from `veldmap-net` via `TileDecodeExecutor`):** Generating `Rgba8UnormSrgb` mip chains on the bounded CPU decode pool (`veldmap-raster::TwoLaneDecodePool` fast lane / `DecodePool`, enforcing both pending-job and pending-byte caps) eliminates grazing-limb aliasing while keeping `GpuTile` strictly `TEXTURE_BINDING | COPY_DST` with exact unpacked byte accounting and keeping `veldmap-net` free of image/raster dependencies. `BundledBasemapProvider` shares this pool with lazy `OnceLock` JPEG decode so `EllipsoidOnly` sessions pay zero decode memory and zero extra OS threads.
- **Why vertically partitioned SQLite WAL (`tile_meta`/`tile_blobs`, `16 KiB` pages) with pooled WAL readers decoupled via `TileBlobStore`:** Whole-source eviction thrashes live basemaps at `z = 12..14`, while loose tile files trigger `relatime` metadata lag and Windows Defender scan storms. Vertically partitioning LRU metadata (`tile_meta`) from raw payloads (`tile_blobs`, linked by `ON DELETE CASCADE`) prevents batched `get()` LRU timestamp flushes from triggering SQLite `OP_MakeRecord` overflow-page rewrites on 256 KiB–1 MiB scientific tiles, while a pooled set of read-only WAL connections allows concurrent reads alongside serialized writes, `PRAGMA user_version` enables clean ephemeral schema upgrades, `(band, format, gutter, decode_rev)` columns prevent multi-band/format aliasing, and `cache_meta` keeps `stats()` O(1). Injecting `veldmap_net::TileBlobStore` decouples `veldmap-net` from SQLite; keeping application settings (`AppSettings`) in `veldmap-app` (flushed asynchronously on `DiskCacheWorker` and drained before join on shutdown) while `veldmap-storage` provides generic durable atomic JSON helpers (`write_json_durable_private()` / `read_json_or_default_with_backup()`) keeps storage infrastructure free of UI/basemap schema coupling and keeps the UI thread free of `fsync` stalls.
- **Why two-stage disk/HTTP scheduling, explicit `PolarCap` contract, enqueue-bounded uploads & deterministic shutdown:** Live Web Mercator servers cover `|lat| <= 85.05112878°` only. Separating disk-lookup concurrency from per-host HTTP permits prevents cached tiles from starving behind slow hosts; enforcing `0` HTTP requests for `PolarCap` keys and `0` requests above `max_level`, routing `PolarCap` and offline fallbacks to `SourceId::BUNDLED_BASEMAP`, enforcing `PendingUploads`'s `max_queued_bytes` cap directly on producer enqueue (shedding finest non-pinned tiles outside the lock so stalled/minimized UI loops never leak RAM), and shutting down in exact order (`CancelToken`/signals & close overlay/vector I/O `SingleThreadWorker` & `DiskCacheWorker` channels → close `PendingUploads` → join `TwoLaneDecodePool` (`fast` then `bulk`) → join overlay & vector I/O workers → shut down `tokio::Runtime` → join `DiskCacheWorker`) prevents polar 404 storms, blank startup flashes, RAM spikes, and exit hangs.

## 7. Safe Positioned I/O, Sampled BLAKE3 Fingerprinting, Bounded GeoTIFF/COG Engine & Two-Lane Decode Pool (Slice 3A-1a)

- **Why synchronous positioned `ByteSource` + `BufferedByteReader` without `memmap2`:** Keeping `ByteSource::read_at` strictly synchronous and caller-buffered (`veldmap-core::io`) lets local positioned-I/O implementations (a single lock-free `File` using `pread(2)` on Unix and pooled `File` handles using `seek_read` on Windows) and future HTTP Range readers share one `Send + Sync` random-access interface with immediate `CancelToken` checking and zero `SIGBUS` UB. Wrapping `ByteSource` in a `64 KiB` `BufferedByteReader` satisfies tiny TIFF header/IFD tag reads and backward seeks inside the buffered window without issuing extra `read_at` syscalls, while bypassing the buffer for large contiguous reads and mapping `IoError::Cancelled` to `ErrorKind::Other` (never `Interrupted`, which `read_exact` retries).
- **Why sampled BLAKE3 (`len || head_64k || mid_64k || tail_64k`) + bounded `(path, len, mtime_ns)` stat cache:** Sampling three disjoint `64 KiB` windows (head, midpoint, tail) plus exact byte length identifies gigabyte-scale GeoTIFF/COG files in `<= 192 KiB` of I/O while providing collision-resistant 256-bit `SourceFingerprint` / `TileDiskCache` keys without external C crypto libraries, and a bounded in-memory `(path, len, mtime_ns)` stat cache (`LocalFingerprintCache`) with post-hash stat validation and explicit bypass (`open_and_fingerprint` with `bypass_cache`) avoids re-hashing unchanged local files on reopen.
- **Why `tiff` crate native `zstd` (`cc`-compiled `zstd-sys`), pooled `Decoder`s & explicit `Limits`/`MAX_IFDS`:** `tiff 0.11.3` only provides ZSTD decompression via its `zstd` feature (`BSD-3-Clause`/`BSD-2-Clause` self-contained `cc` build with zero external system-library setup, consistent with §2). Pooling opened `Decoder<BufferedByteReader>` handles (rebinding each caller's `CancelToken` via `BufferedByteReader::set_cancel`) avoids re-reading TIFF headers and re-walking IFD chains on every pointwise cache miss, while explicit `tiff::decoder::Limits`, bounds-checked chunk queries, `MAX_IFDS = 64`, `MAX_SOURCE_SIDE = 65_536` for untiled/stripped rasters (allowing larger tiled COGs/BigTIFFs whose chunk grid and `LevelPlan`s fit `MemoryBudget`), `Predictor = 3` (Float32) + `GeoDoubleParamsTag`/`GeoAsciiParamsTag` parsing, and `ExtraSamples` Gray+Alpha recognition let `8/16`-bit and `f32` chunky (`PlanarConfiguration = 1`) non-palette/non-YCbCr GeoTIFF/BigTIFF/ZSTD-COG files decode safely (`YCbCr` and `WebP` landed in Slice 3A-2 (§9); `PlanarConfiguration = 2`, 4-band RGB+NIR `ExtraSamples`, and `Palette` remain deferred to Slice 3B).
- **Why non-cached sequential passes, leader-cancel-safe singleflight & two-lane decoding:** Running pointwise reads through `SharedChunkCache` (bounded LRU + panic/cancel-safe singleflight preserving full `RasterError` variants directly via `Arc`-backed error payloads and letting uncancelled followers take over cleanly if a leader cancels via its own token) while letting `TiffSource::produce_pass` decode base-IFD strips/tiles sequentially *outside* `SharedChunkCache` prevents AB-BA deadlocks between a large pass working-memory lease and concurrent pointwise leaders and keeps full-file passes from evicting pointwise chunks. `TwoLaneDecodePool` isolates `Fast` (pointwise) and `Bulk` (pass/full-image) lanes inside `veldmap-raster` (keyed by typed `DecodeJobKey::{Probe, Tile, Pass}`, wired into `SceneState` for both `BasemapManager` and `OverlayManager`), keeping local GeoTIFFs independent of `veldmap-net` while eliminating head-of-line stalls behind slow passes.
- **Why pure `MemoryBudget` math in `veldmap-core` + OS RAM probing in `veldmap-storage` + `StreamKey`-scoped `TileScheduler` in `veldmap-raster`:** Keeping `MemoryBudget::for_host_ram` and deterministic `Default` (8 GiB fallback) pure in `veldmap-core` while probing `/proc/meminfo` on Linux, `sysctl` on macOS, and `GlobalMemoryStatusEx` on Windows (added in Slice 3A-1b) in `veldmap-storage::detect_memory_budget` (wired into `VeldmapApp`/`SceneState`) preserves `veldmap-core`'s zero-OS-I/O boundary and makes unit tests deterministic across machines. Deriving independent per-cache caps once at startup avoids lock-reentrant cross-cache shrinking, while `DecodeBudgetTracker` enforces FIFO waiter queuing so small pointwise leases never starve large sequential passes. `TileScheduler` (co-located with the raster pipeline in `veldmap-raster`, wired into `OverlayManager` in Slice 3A-1b) enforces 1 active pass per `StreamKey` (`source`, `band`, `format`, `gutter`; originally named `PassKey`) strictly for `ServeMode::Pass`, runs `ServeMode::Pointwise` concurrently, preserves coarser or shared-cascade passes on zoom changes, and separates permanent rejections from once-forgiven single-pass transient failures without starving pass-only sources.

## 8. Depth-Less Post-Body Warp-Mesh Draping, Normal-Facing Horizon Clip & QOI Cache (Slice 3A-1b)

- **Why two-pass offscreen rendering (opaque `Depth32Float` Pass 1 + depth-less Pass 2):** Closing Pass 1 after the opaque globe body keeps `Depth32Float` (`TRANSIENT_ATTACHMENT` + `StoreOp::Discard`) memoryless on TBDR GPUs, while rendering draped overlays and the procedural graticule in a `depth_stencil_attachment: None` color pass (`LoadOp::Load`) eliminates chord-sag Z-fighting across independently triangulated meshes.
- **Why a frame-wide `DynamicUniformArena` & single `TileBindGroupCache`:** Staging all aligned Pass 1 + Pass 2 dynamic uniforms into one arena flushed once per dirty frame prevents `queue.write_buffer(..., 0, ...)` clobbering across passes. Keeping `TileBindGroupCache` as a single `Weak<GpuTile>`-guarded map (keyed by unique `GpuTile::tile_id` and guarded on `PixelFormat::Rgba8UnormSrgb`) avoids speculative dead layouts until Slice 3B adds `R32Float` / `Uint` pipelines.
- **Why outward vertex-normal horizon facing (`dot(in.normal, -in.world) <= 0.0`) instead of a bare tangent-plane clip:** A bare horizon-plane clip (`P · towards >= horizon`) fails directly beneath a nadir camera whenever flat-chord sag exceeds altitude (e.g. `121 m` sag on `0.5°` segments at `2 m` altitude). Evaluating `dot(in.normal, -in.world) <= 0.0` (matching `globe.wgsl::fs_grid`) guarantees zero holes at `MIN_HEIGHT_M = 2.0 m` even on global single-tile overlays while clipping back-hemisphere limb fragments strictly inside the convex ellipsoid silhouette. Uniform per-level `level_segments` (`16..=64`) eliminates T-junction cracks between adjacent cells (including clipped edges), outward volume checks flip winding for negative-determinant/south-up affines, and lazy `5 × 5`-probe `OverlayCullTree` balls (`veldmap-core::tile::overlay`, keeping `geo` acyclic) delegate visibility directly to `CullFrustum::admits`.
- **Why keep QOI codec helpers in `veldmap-raster`, off-UI-thread overlay I/O & native Windows RAM detection:** Keeping `encode_qoi_rgba8`/`decode_qoi_rgba8` (pure-Rust `qoi` crate, symmetric `16 MiB` bounds + sRGB/RGBA validation) inside `veldmap-raster` while `TileDiskCache` provides ABA-safe `get_encoded_entry`/`delete_entry` and cancellation-checked `put_encoded_batch_if_uncancelled` keeps `veldmap-storage` strictly codec-free. Running local file open/reload + `clear_source` on a dedicated overlay I/O `SingleThreadWorker` (`spawn_overlay_io_worker`) with versioned `(job_id, CancelToken)` pointwise scheduling prevents UI stalls and reload cache poisoning; native Windows `GlobalMemoryStatusEx` closes the last OS gap in `detect_memory_budget()`.

## 9. Pure-Rust CRS/WKT Expansion, Drone RGB Codecs, Subdivided Pass-2 Vectors & Workstation UX (Slice 3A-2)

- **Why hybrid in-house Krueger + `proj4rs`/`proj4wkt`/`crs-definitions` (and explicit `HelmertConvention`):** Retaining our fast exact 4th-order Krueger Transverse Mercator (`System`, extended with `origin_lat_deg` and `unit_to_metres` for International/US Survey Feet) and Web Mercator alongside pure-Rust `proj4rs` + `proj4wkt` + `crs-definitions` (`MIT`/`Apache-2.0`/`CC0-1.0`, zero C `libproj` deps) gives zero-overhead precision for common UTM/GK/MSK-xx rasters while covering arbitrary 2D EPSG codes and WKT1/WKT2 `.prj` sidecars. `Proj4Projection` rejects 3D geocentric CRSs (`is_geocent()`), falls back to a 3-parameter NAD27 shift with `DatumWarning::GridShiftRecommended` when optional `+nadgrids` files are absent, and inspects `ellipse_parameters()` so any unshifted non-WGS84 ellipsoid raises `UnshiftedForeignDatum`. Tagging `Datum::convention` explicitly as `HelmertConvention::CoordinateFrame` (EPSG:9607) vs. `PositionVector` (EPSG:9606 / WKT `TOWGS84`) prevents silent sign-flip bugs across datum sources, and wiring `Datum::to_wgs84`/`from_wgs84` into geographic `Frame` bindings shifts foreign-datum geographic rasters accurately.
- **Why direct TIFF `YCbCr -> RGBA8`, pure-Rust `webp`, `jpeg-decoder` over `zune-jpeg`, `BufRead` on `BufferedByteReader` & injected sidecars:** `tiff 0.11.3` disables `zune-jpeg`'s internal YCbCr color conversion so `ColorType::YCbCr(8)` always delivers raw upsampled YCbCr bytes; converting directly to RGBA8 in `tiff/format.rs` / `tiff/ycbcr.rs` using `YCbCrCoefficients` (tag 529, BT.601 default) and `ReferenceBlackWhite` (tag 532)—with per-IFD YCbCr tracking so YCbCr overviews on RGB GeoTIFFs work cleanly—avoids intermediate 3 B/px copies while `tiff`'s pure-Rust `webp` feature (`0xC351`) covers WebP-compressed drone COGs with zero C codecs. `JpegSource` intentionally keeps `jpeg-decoder` (`default-features = false`, zero transitive deps) over `zune-jpeg` because `jpeg-decoder` supports DCT `scale()` downscaling and lossless/16-bit JPEGs, while enforcing full-resolution coefficient/sample peak accounting on progressive/lossless scans and upfront `set_max_decoding_buffer_size` limits. Implementing `std::io::BufRead` directly on `BufferedByteReader` lets `PngSource` stream non-interlaced rows directly into `Cascade` (enforcing upfront `png::Limits` and whole-frame budget caps on Adam7 PNGs), and loading `.tfw`/`.pgw`/`.jgw`/`.wld` + `.prj` sidecars at the opener seam keeps every raster decoder pure `ByteSource`.
- **Why exterior-pole stereographic projection, Conforming Midpoint Closure, per-cluster RTC & GPU-instanced points in Pass 2:** Projecting arbitrary spherical polygons via conformal stereographic projection from a point strictly outside the polygon handles polar, antimeridian, and `> 180°` (>hemisphere) polygons without hemisphere-splitting seams or pole blowups. Triangulating with pure-Rust `earcut`, subdividing edges longer than `max_chord_deg` via stereographic midpoints (`<= 0.5°`), running a read-only Conforming Midpoint Closure pass (`close_t_junctions`) to eliminate T-junction cracks along shared edges, and packing per-cluster `f64` RTC origins (`VectorCluster`) into shared layer vertex buffers preserves sub-millimetre precision at `2 m` zoom for drone-scale overlays alongside continental geometries. Rendering fills, strokes, and zoom-invariant GPU-instanced point markers (`vs_point`, `VertexStepMode::Instance`) in Pass 2 (`shaders/vector.wgsl`) reuses `DynamicUniformArena`'s 32-byte dynamic uniform slot (`VectorUniform`) and `dot(in.normal, -in.world) <= 0.0` horizon clipping with zero CPU mesh rebuilds on zoom.
- **Why main-thread `rfd::AsyncFileDialog`, `dejavu` fallback & coalesced `session.json`:** Constructing `rfd::AsyncFileDialog::pick_files()` / `save_file()` on the UI thread with `.set_parent(frame)` (required for non-blocking AppKit sheets on macOS) and polling the returned `Send` future off-thread via `pollster::block_on` avoids UI freezes on all 3 OSes (`rfd 0.18` `xdg-portal` + `wayland` avoids `gtk3`/`glib` build deps on Linux, opening `libdbus-1` via `dlopen` when a portal is present and keeping drag-and-drop / `Path…` / CLI args as fallbacks). Layering `dejavu::sans::regular()` and `dejavu::sans_mono::regular()` behind `egui`'s defaults provides deterministic cross-OS non-Latin and GIS symbol coverage without checking font binaries into git. Persisting `session.json` via `write_json_durable_private()` with write-coalescing on `DiskCacheWorker` prevents UI `fsync` stalls and drains cleanly on shutdown.

## 10. Scientific & Categorical GPU Tracks, Direct Raw-Chunk GeoTIFFs, Byte-Shuffled `Lz4Raw` Cache & Live Stretch (Slice 3B)

- **Why keep `tiff 0.11.3` strictly for open-time IFD parsing + direct raw-chunk decoding over `ByteSource`:** `tiff 0.11.3`'s `Decoder::read_chunk` unconditionally rejects `PhotometricInterpretation = 3` (`Palette`), truncates 4-band RGB+NIR (`ExtraSamples = 0`) tiles and corrupts their predictors, and fails on tiled `PlanarConfiguration = 2` NPOT bottom-edge chunks. Parsing and caching bounded per-IFD tables (`TileOffsets`/`StripOffsets`, `TileByteCounts`/`StripByteCounts`, `JPEGTables`, `ColorMap`, YCbCr params, and layout—bounded against `MemoryBudget` and `MAX_SINGLE_CHUNK_BYTES = 256 MiB`) once in `TiffSource::open` and decoding raw chunks lock-free over `ByteSource` using our already-locked codecs (`flate2`, `weezl`, `zstd`, `jpeg-decoder`, `image-webp`) resolves all three blockers with zero new codec crates, eliminates pooled stateful `Decoder` contention, validates JPEG/WebP decoded chunk dimensions and `PixelFormat::RGB24` YCbCr conversion cleanly, and directly exposes the cached IFD offset tables needed by Slice 4A remote COG range prefetching.
- **Why three typed GPU tracks (`Rgba8UnormSrgb`, `R32Float` with NaN-skipping mips, `R8Uint`/`R16Uint` 1-mip) and `f32` display vs `f64` CPU readout:** Generalizing `RasterSource`, `DecodedChunk`, `Grid`, `Cascade`, and `driver::{direct, pass}` by `TileStreamSpec`/`PixelFormat` gates `bleed_alpha` strictly to `Rgba8UnormSrgb` so scientific and categorical samples are never smeared across nodata edges. `R32Float` tiles (`512 × 512`, `gutter = 0`, `~1.33 MiB` with full CPU NaN-skipping box-filter mipmaps including NPOT edge tiles) store raw numeric values with nodata/non-finite mapped to `f32::NAN`, while `R8Uint` / `R16Uint` categorical tiles (`gutter = 0`, 1 mip, nearest-neighbor downsampling) share `texture_2d<u32>` bindings without blending class IDs. Because IEEE-754 `f32` has a 24-bit mantissa (~7 decimal digits), GPU `R32Float` visualization quantizes 32-bit integers (`u32`/`i32`) and `f64` rasters beyond `2^24`; zoom-invariant CPU cursor readouts (`RasterSource::sample_band`) therefore read native precision in `f64` directly from the base IFD and evaluate canonical `raw * scale + offset` (matching GDAL `GDAL_METADATA` / STAC `raster:bands` / CF NetCDF).
- **Why manual 8-tap nodata-aware trilinear WGSL `textureLoad` + `bitcast<u32>` finiteness check + 256-entry `PaletteUniform`:** Sampling `R32Float` via hardware linear samplers requires the optional `FLOAT32_FILTERABLE` feature (absent on baseline WebGPU / `downlevel_defaults`) and smears `NaN` across 2×2 quads. Computing `dpdx`/`dpdy` mip LOD strictly in uniform control flow *before* horizon `discard` and evaluating two per-mip 4-tap `textureLoad(tile_tex, coord, mip)` bilinear taps (indexed against `textureDimensions(tile_tex, mip)` for NPOT tiles) eliminates nearest-mip zoom popping and minification shimmer without `FLOAT32_FILTERABLE`. Checking `(bitcast<u32>(v) & 0x7F800000u) != 0x7F800000u` strictly before any arithmetic prevents Metal fast-math optimizations from collapsing `NaN`/`±Inf` checks. Sharing one 4096-byte `PaletteUniform` (`256 × vec4<f32>` linear-light stops, well within baseline 16 KiB UBO limits) across both continuous scientific colormaps (linear and `10·log10` dB stretch) and categorical palettes (`SourceColorMap`, ESA Sentinel-2 `SCL`, deterministic hash) keeps both shaders within 4 bind groups.
- **Why byte-shuffled `lz4_flex` (`TileBlobEncoding::Lz4Raw`) disk caching:** Storing mip-0 `R32Float`/`R8Uint`/`R16Uint` tiles in `TileDiskCache` with a 24-byte self-describing `VML4` header (`magic`, `version`, `format`, `gutter`, `flags`, `width`, `height`, `valid_w`, `valid_h`, deriving `expected_bytes = width * height * bpp` before allocation), 4-byte / 2-byte lane shuffling (grouping float exponent/mantissa bytes for markedly higher LZ4 compression ratios), pure-Rust checked `lz4_flex` (MIT, zero `unsafe`), and an explicit pre-allocation bounds guard matches `Qoi` durability while regenerating `R32Float` mip chains on disk-cache hit.
- **Why `StreamKey`-keyed `UploadBatchReport`, hoisted multi-format `TileBindGroupCache` & multi-band VRAM budgeting:** Keying upload byte reports by `StreamKey` prevents `R32Float` (`~1.33 MiB`) and `R8Uint` (`256 KiB`) streams sharing one `SourceId` from mis-weighting each other's VRAM appetite. Hoisting `TileBindGroupCache` into `egui_wgpu::CallbackResources` (keyed by unique `GpuTile::tile_id` with `Weak<GpuTile>` sweeping across `Rgba8UnormSrgb`, `R32Float`, and `R8Uint`/`R16Uint` bind-group layouts) prepares clean texture sharing for future 2D orthographic / band-math views, while summing active stream weights across multi-stream `OverlayLayer`s, supporting opt-in `pin_root`, and exposing an optional `128–4096 MiB` GPU cache cap in Settings keeps multi-band EO working sets responsive without throttling into coarse overviews.
