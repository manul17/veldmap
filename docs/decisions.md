# Design Decisions

Concise record of **implemented** architectural decisions—focusing on *why alternatives were rejected* and *hard cross-slice invariants* that cannot be inferred from reading the code alone.

> **Policy:** Record a decision here only once it is implemented and proven in code (or when preserving a hard numerical/safety invariant). Unbuilt future slices live as working hypotheses in [plan.md](../plan.md), not pre-accepted contracts. Module implementation details live in Rust `//!` docstrings (`cargo doc`).

---

## 1. Native Rust Workspace & UI/GPU-Free Domain Core

- **Why not WASI / Wasmtime / protobuf IPC:** VeldMap's legacy prototype routed components through a WASI/Wasmtime event bus with protobuf serialization, adding IPC overhead, `!Send` constraints, and 32-bit guest memory limits with no desktop benefit. `wasmtime`, `prost`, and `tonic` are banned in [deny.toml](../deny.toml) to prevent regressions.
- **Why keep `veldmap-core` free of UI/GPU crates:** Keeping `egui`, `eframe`, and `wgpu` out of `veldmap-core` ensures fast headless builds/tests without graphics drivers or windowing libraries and preserves reuse in future companions.
- **Workspace crate growth:** Split workspace crates along real dependency seams (`veldmap-core` pure leaf, `veldmap-raster` landed in Slice 2A, `veldmap-storage` and `veldmap-net` landed in Slice 2B) rather than simulating crate boundaries via feature flags.

## 2. Dependency Hygiene & Safety Defaults

- **No `memmap2` for user files (banned in [deny.toml](../deny.toml)):** Memory-mapped I/O in Rust risks `SIGBUS` UB on truncated, removable, or network-backed files. Positioned I/O (`pread`/`seek_read`) with caller buffers is safe, portable, and mirrors HTTP Range reads. (Wayland SHM wrappers `smithay-client-toolkit` and `winit` remain exempted.)
- **TLS via `rustls` + `ring`, no OpenSSL or heavy ORMs:** `openssl`/`openssl-sys` and `sqlx` are banned in [deny.toml](../deny.toml) to avoid cross-OS TLS linking issues and heavyweight ORM plumbing. `veldmap-net` explicitly configures `reqwest` with `rustls-no-provider` and `rustls`'s `ring` provider (compiled via `cc` with zero external CMake/NASM build tools while satisfying `deny.toml` SPDX checks).
- **No external C geospatial libraries (`gdal`/`proj`); self-contained bundled SQLite & ZSTD:** Default builds avoid external C system libraries (`gdal`, `proj`, `libhdf5`) and keep `veldmap-core` permanently pure-Rust, while self-contained `cc`-compiled crates (`ring` for TLS, `rusqlite` `bundled` for the ephemeral tile cache §6, and `zstd-sys` via the `zstd` crate in `veldmap-raster` §7/§10) remain allowed when justified.

## 3. Smooth WGS84 Ellipsoid (`height = 0.0`), Reverse-Z & 2 m Floor

- **Why no 3D DEM mesh displacement in v1:** Orthorectified optical satellite imagery and cm-scale drone orthophotos place pixels at true geodetic `(lat, lon)`. Draping imagery onto a mismatched DEM (e.g., 30 m COP-DEM over a cm-scale drone orthophoto built on its own DSM) shifts pixels horizontally by `Δh × tan(θ)` (orders of magnitude above `< 2 mm`), introduces view-dependent relief parallax, conflicts with zero-resampling native-CRS warp meshes, and requires terrain-ray intersections, elevated shadow-cone horizon culling, depth-tested vector draping, and EGM2008 vertical-datum conversion. A flat, map-consistent ellipsoid is therefore the right **analytical default** (swipe, diff, readout, 2D/3D sync); terrain relief will be delivered as quantitative GPU layers (hillshade, slope, aspect; Slice 5B-1), and any future v2 3D terrain relief belongs in a separate view mode rather than replacing the flat surface.
- **Current nadir camera:** VeldMap v1 uses a nadir camera (`Camera`) for uniform center GSD and simple controls; zero camera tilt is a v1 UX simplification (not physically required to avoid relief parallax on a flat `height = 0.0` surface). Because v1 `Camera`, `cull::CullFrustum`, `geodesy::surface_at`, and depth-less draped primitives optimize for `height_m = 0.0`, any future v2 3D terrain or oblique mode will extend or replace those modules rather than layering transparently via synchronous height-oracle traits.
- **Why `MIN_HEIGHT_M = 2.0` m with `f64` infinite Reverse-Z & depth-less overlays:** The legacy renderer floated draped lines on an 80 m `SURFACE_LIFT_M` to clear coarse mesh chord sag, forcing an 800 m camera floor (~0.75 m/px at 1000 rows, 50° FOV—37× too coarse for 2 cm drone orthophotos). Moreover, independently triangulated smooth-ellipsoid meshes (e.g., a coarse native-CRS warp mesh over a finer basemap quadtree) have different chord sagittas and clip or Z-fight if depth-tested against each other. Rendering the draped graticule and overlay passes back-to-front **depth-less** (`depth_write: false`, `depth_compare: Always` or `depth_stencil_attachment: None` post-body passes) with fragment horizon discard removes `SURFACE_LIFT_M`, lowers the floor to `2.0` m (~1.87 mm/px at 1000 rows, 50° FOV), and keeps `Depth32Float` (`RENDER_ATTACHMENT | TRANSIENT_ATTACHMENT` + `StoreOp::Discard`) memoryless on TBDR GPUs without `COPY_SRC` depth readback.
- **Exact 1:1 sRGB compositing:** Offscreen rendering targets `Rgba8UnormSrgb` with `view_formats: &[Rgba8Unorm]` and linear RGB constants, compositing 1:1 via `FilterMode::Nearest` using the view matching the swapchain color space (zero manual `encode_srgb` hacks).

## 4. Overlay Accuracy & Readout Contract (Cross-Slice Benchmark)

Raster warp-mesh placement (`a_raster_lands_where_its_binding_puts_it`, enforced since Slice 3A-1) verifies surface placement (including `f32` vertex encoding) across all 5 benchmark bindings in UTM zone 37 (`EPSG:32637`):

1. Drone orthophoto at **2 cm/px**
2. Sentinel-2 granule at **10 m/px**
3. Landsat scene at **30 m/px**
4. Sentinel-2 granule at **pyramid level 4**
5. **8000×5000** orthophoto **rotated by 30°**

Tolerances (Slice 3A-1 benchmark):
- `< 10⁻³` of the pixel size of the drawn pyramid level
- `< 2 mm` for any raster drawn at its native resolution
- `< 2 cm` at any UTM-scale pyramid level (coarse regional/global cells wider than `~8°` cap subdivision at `0.5°/segment`, staying strictly `< 10⁻³` px)
- Paired with additional CRS fixtures (`EPSG:28407`, `EPSG:32737`, `EPSG:3857`) and an end-to-end **`PixelIsArea` vs. `PixelIsPoint` (`GTRasterTypeGeoKey`)** half-pixel check.

Cross-slice numerical & readout invariants:
- **CPU placement + close-up GPU verification:** Assert `< 10⁻³` px placement on CPU exact geometry and texture coordinates (GPU bilinear filtering is typically 8-bit / `1/256` texel), paired with close-up (`2 m`) between-vertices GPU smoke tests verifying cell-midpoint sag and LOD continuity.
- **Display sampling ≠ scientific readout:** Cursor clicks identify the visible geographic location; scientific cursor readouts and time-series queries must read native source datasets on CPU with explicit resolution, interpolation, and nodata rules so values remain zoom-invariant as sharper display tiles stream in.

## 5. `f64` ECEF Metres, Geodetic Nadir Aim & Decoupled Quadtree Geometry (Slice 2A)

- **Why `f64` ECEF metres + per-tile CPU `f64` RTC (instead of normalized units and GPU double-single RTE):** Expressing ECEF directly in metres unifies `geodesy::Ellipsoid` (Fukushima 2006 3D inverse) with `projection::Datum` Helmert shifts and eliminates scale-factor conversions across `Camera`, `cull::CullFrustum`, and tile geometry. Computing `(tile_origin - eye)` on CPU in `f64` and storing tile vertices relative to `tile_origin` in `f32` preserves ~24 µm precision at `2 m` altitude without relying on non-reassociating GPU float math across Vulkan/DX12/Metal compilers.
- **Why geodetic nadir aim (`forward = -self.at`):** Aiming geocentrically toward Earth's center introduces a `~0.192°` mid-latitude offset (~3.6 px at 1000 rows) and `~14.7 km` orbit-to-2 m center-zoom drift. Aiming along `-self.at` makes `Camera::probe(0.5, 0.5, aspect)` agree identically with `Camera::center()` across all altitudes and latitudes.
- **Why decoupled geometry vs. texture LOD (`z_geom <= 16–17`) + adaptive Polar Stereographic caps:** Basemaps cap out at `max_level`, whereas `2 m` viewing requires sub-millimetre chord sag. Subdividing geometry to `z_geom = 16–17` while clamping texture requests to `<= max_tex_level` and sampling ancestors guarantees `0` requests above `max_tex_level` and `< 1 mm` sag at `2 m`. Covering `|lat| >= 85.05112878°` with elliptical square-to-disk Polar Stereographic quadtree caps eliminates polar slivers and antimeridian UV seams while stitching seamlessly via sag-scaled outward-flared skirts and 2:1 neighbor balancing.
- **Why post-overlay procedural `fwidth()` graticule & `GpuTile` hygiene:** Evaluating the graticule procedurally in a transparent post-overlay pass (with uniform-flow `fwidth()` strictly before horizon `discard`) removes chord parallax and prevents raster overlays from burying it. Keeping `GpuTile` strictly limited to immutable texture/view/format/dimensions (`valid_w`, `valid_h`, `gutter`)/byte-size/ID—with `TileCache` delegating ancestor walks to `TileScheme::parent` (exposing UV-decoupled `find_carrier` for clipped/guttered `RasterPyramid` tiles) and `GlobeRenderer` sweeping `(Weak<GpuTile>, wgpu::BindGroup)` entries on `prepare()`—lets one cache contract serve planetary basemaps and local/cloud raster pyramids without inverting parent direction or leaking GPU textures across discarded `egui` passes.

## 6. Async Tile Streaming, CPU Linear-Light Mipmaps, Vertically Partitioned SQLite WAL Cache & Live Basemaps (Slice 2B)

- **Why zero-dep async-waking `CancelToken` and `Wakeup` in `veldmap-core::sync`:** Keeping `CancelToken`, `Wakeup`, and `lock_unpoisoned` in `veldmap-core::sync` (with `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` across `veldmap-core`) lets synchronous raster decoders, disk-cache workers, local `ByteSource`s, and `tokio::select!` HTTP fetches share cancellation and repaint-wakeup primitives without leaking `tokio` into `veldmap-core` or `veldmap-net` into storage/bundled-basemap workers.
- **Why CPU linear-light mipmaps + `TwoLaneDecodePool` / `DecodePool` in `veldmap-raster` (decoupled from `veldmap-net` via `TileDecodeExecutor`):** Generating `Rgba8UnormSrgb` mip chains on the bounded CPU decode pool (`veldmap-raster::TwoLaneDecodePool` fast lane / `DecodePool`, enforcing both pending-job and pending-byte caps) eliminates grazing-limb aliasing while keeping `GpuTile` strictly `TEXTURE_BINDING | COPY_DST` with exact unpacked byte accounting and keeping `veldmap-net` free of image/raster dependencies. `BundledBasemapProvider` shares this pool with lazy `OnceLock` JPEG decode so `EllipsoidOnly` sessions pay zero decode memory and zero extra OS threads.
- **Why vertically partitioned SQLite WAL (`tile_meta`/`tile_blobs`, `16 KiB` pages) with pooled WAL readers decoupled via `TileBlobStore`:** Whole-source eviction thrashes live basemaps at `z = 12..14`, while loose tile files trigger `relatime` metadata lag and Windows Defender scan storms. Vertically partitioning LRU metadata (`tile_meta`) from raw payloads (`tile_blobs`, linked by `ON DELETE CASCADE`) prevents batched `get()` LRU timestamp flushes from triggering SQLite `OP_MakeRecord` overflow-page rewrites on 256 KiB–1 MiB scientific tiles, while a pooled set of read-only WAL connections allows concurrent reads alongside serialized writes, `PRAGMA user_version` enables clean ephemeral schema upgrades, `(band, format, gutter, decode_rev)` columns prevent multi-band/format aliasing, and `cache_meta` keeps `stats()` O(1). Injecting `veldmap_net::TileBlobStore` decouples `veldmap-net` from SQLite; keeping application settings (`AppSettings`) in `veldmap-app` (flushed asynchronously on `DiskCacheWorker` and drained before join on shutdown) while `veldmap-storage` provides generic durable atomic JSON helpers (`write_json_durable_private()` / `read_json_or_default_with_backup()`) keeps storage infrastructure free of UI/basemap schema coupling and keeps the UI thread free of `fsync` stalls.
- **Why two-stage disk/HTTP scheduling, explicit `PolarCap` contract, enqueue-bounded uploads & deterministic shutdown:** Live Web Mercator servers cover `|lat| <= 85.05112878°` only. Separating disk-lookup concurrency from per-host HTTP permits prevents cached tiles from starving behind slow hosts; enforcing `0` HTTP requests for `PolarCap` keys and `0` requests above `max_level`, routing `PolarCap` and offline fallbacks to `SourceId::BUNDLED_BASEMAP`, enforcing `PendingUploads`'s `max_queued_bytes` cap directly on producer enqueue (shedding finest non-pinned tiles outside the lock so stalled/minimized UI loops never leak RAM), and shutting down in exact order (persist final `session.json` → Phase 1: cancel overlay/vector/basemap/STAC/`decode_shutdown` tokens, `NetContext::signal_shutdown()`, and close overlay I/O, vector I/O, `veldmap-tile-write`, & `DiskCacheWorker` channels → Phase 2: close `PendingUploads` and join `veldmap-png-export` (`png_export.join_background_encode()`) → Phase 3: join `TwoLaneDecodePool` (`fast` then `bulk`), join `veldmap-remote-io`, overlay I/O, `veldmap-tile-write`, & vector I/O workers, shut down `LiveTilePipeline` → shut down `NetContext` (`tokio::Runtime`) → join `DiskCacheWorker`) prevents polar 404 storms, blank startup flashes, RAM spikes, and exit hangs.

## 7. Safe Positioned I/O, Sampled BLAKE3 Fingerprinting, Bounded GeoTIFF/COG Engine & Two-Lane Decode Pool (Slice 3A-1a)

- **Why synchronous positioned `ByteSource` + `BufferedByteReader` without `memmap2`:** Keeping `ByteSource::read_at` strictly synchronous and caller-buffered (`veldmap-core::io`) lets local positioned-I/O implementations (a single lock-free `File` using `pread(2)` on Unix and pooled `File` handles using `seek_read` on Windows) and HTTP Range readers (`HttpRangeSource`, §11) share one `Send + Sync` random-access interface with immediate `CancelToken` checking and zero `SIGBUS` UB. Wrapping `ByteSource` in a `64 KiB` `BufferedByteReader` satisfies tiny header/tag reads and backward seeks inside the buffered window without issuing extra `read_at` syscalls, while bypassing the buffer for large contiguous reads and mapping `IoError::Cancelled` to `ErrorKind::Other` (never `Interrupted`, which `read_exact` retries).
- **Why sampled BLAKE3 (`len || head_64k || mid_64k || tail_64k`) + bounded `(path, len, mtime_ns)` stat cache:** Sampling three disjoint `64 KiB` windows (head, midpoint, tail) plus exact byte length identifies gigabyte-scale GeoTIFF/COG files in `<= 192 KiB` of I/O while providing collision-resistant 256-bit `SourceFingerprint` / `TileDiskCache` keys without external C crypto libraries, and a bounded in-memory `(path, len, mtime_ns)` stat cache (`LocalFingerprintCache`) with post-hash stat validation and explicit bypass (`open_and_fingerprint` with `bypass_cache`) avoids re-hashing unchanged local files on reopen.
- **Why `zstd-sys` (`cc`-compiled) & explicit IFD/chunk bounds (Slice 3A-1a; evolved in §10–§11):** Slice 3A-1a initially enabled `tiff 0.11.3`'s `zstd` feature (`BSD-3-Clause`/`BSD-2-Clause` self-contained `cc` build, consistent with §2) with pooled `Decoder<BufferedByteReader>` handles; Slice 3B (§10) replaced `Decoder::read_chunk` with lock-free direct raw-chunk decoding calling `zstd` directly, and Slice 4A (§11) replaced open-time `tiff::Decoder` with `SinglePassIfdReader` (moving `tiff` out of production dependencies into `test-support`/dev-only). Explicit bounds-checked chunk queries, `MAX_IFDS = 64`, `MAX_SOURCE_SIDE = 65_536` for untiled/stripped rasters (allowing larger tiled COGs/BigTIFFs whose chunk grid and `LevelPlan`s fit `MemoryBudget`), `Predictor = 3` (Float32) + `GeoDoubleParamsTag`/`GeoAsciiParamsTag` parsing, and `ExtraSamples` Gray+Alpha recognition landed here (`YCbCr`/`WebP` followed in §9, and `PlanarConfiguration = 2`, 4-band RGB+NIR `ExtraSamples`, and `Palette` landed in §10).
- **Why non-cached sequential passes, leader-cancel-safe singleflight & two-lane decoding:** Running pointwise reads through `SharedChunkCache` (bounded LRU + panic/cancel-safe singleflight preserving full `RasterError` variants directly via `Arc`-backed error payloads and letting uncancelled followers take over cleanly if a leader cancels via its own token) while letting `TiffSource::produce_pass` decode base-IFD strips/tiles sequentially *outside* `SharedChunkCache` prevents AB-BA deadlocks between a large pass working-memory lease and concurrent pointwise leaders and keeps full-file passes from evicting pointwise chunks. `TwoLaneDecodePool` isolates `Fast` (pointwise) and `Bulk` (pass/full-image) lanes inside `veldmap-raster` (keyed by typed `DecodeJobKey::{Open, Probe, BandStats, Tile, Pass}`, wired into `SceneState` for both `BasemapManager` and `OverlayManager`), keeping local GeoTIFFs independent of `veldmap-net` while eliminating head-of-line stalls behind slow passes.
- **Why pure `MemoryBudget` math in `veldmap-core` + OS RAM probing in `veldmap-storage` + `StreamKey`-scoped `TileScheduler` in `veldmap-raster`:** Keeping `MemoryBudget::for_host_ram` and deterministic `Default` (8 GiB fallback) pure in `veldmap-core` while probing `/proc/meminfo` on Linux, `sysctl` on macOS, and `GlobalMemoryStatusEx` on Windows (added in Slice 3A-1b) in `veldmap-storage::detect_memory_budget` (wired into `VeldmapApp`/`SceneState`) preserves `veldmap-core`'s zero-OS-I/O boundary and makes unit tests deterministic across machines. Deriving independent per-cache caps once at startup avoids lock-reentrant cross-cache shrinking, while `DecodeBudgetTracker` enforces FIFO waiter queuing so small pointwise leases never starve large sequential passes. `TileScheduler` (co-located with the raster pipeline in `veldmap-raster`, wired into `OverlayManager` in Slice 3A-1b) enforces 1 active pass per `StreamKey` (`source`, `band`, `format`, `gutter`; originally named `PassKey`) strictly for `ServeMode::Pass`, runs `ServeMode::Pointwise` concurrently, preserves coarser or shared-cascade passes on zoom changes, and separates permanent rejections from once-forgiven single-pass transient failures without starving pass-only sources.

## 8. Depth-Less Post-Body Warp-Mesh Draping, Normal-Facing Horizon Clip & QOI Cache (Slice 3A-1b)

- **Why two-pass offscreen rendering (opaque `Depth32Float` Pass 1 + depth-less Pass 2):** Closing Pass 1 after the opaque globe body keeps `Depth32Float` (`TRANSIENT_ATTACHMENT` + `StoreOp::Discard`) memoryless on TBDR GPUs, while rendering draped overlays and the procedural graticule in a `depth_stencil_attachment: None` color pass (`LoadOp::Load`) eliminates chord-sag Z-fighting across independently triangulated meshes.
- **Why a frame-wide `DynamicUniformArena` & single `TileBindGroupCache`:** Staging all aligned Pass 1 + Pass 2 dynamic uniforms into one arena flushed once per dirty frame prevents `queue.write_buffer(..., 0, ...)` clobbering across passes. Keeping `TileBindGroupCache` as a single `Weak<GpuTile>`-guarded map keyed by unique `GpuTile::tile_id` initially avoided speculative dead layouts in Slice 3A-1b and expanded cleanly to multi-format (`Rgba8UnormSrgb`, `R32Float`, `R8Uint`/`R16Uint`) and `CallbackResources` hoisting in §10.
- **Why outward vertex-normal horizon facing (`dot(in.normal, -in.world) <= 0.0`) instead of a bare tangent-plane clip:** A bare horizon-plane clip (`P · towards >= horizon`) fails directly beneath a nadir camera whenever flat-chord sag exceeds altitude (e.g. `121 m` sag on `0.5°` segments at `2 m` altitude). Evaluating `dot(in.normal, -in.world) <= 0.0` (matching `globe.wgsl::fs_grid`) guarantees zero holes at `MIN_HEIGHT_M = 2.0 m` even on global single-tile overlays while clipping back-hemisphere limb fragments strictly inside the convex ellipsoid silhouette. Uniform per-level `level_segments` (`16..=64`) eliminates T-junction cracks between adjacent cells (including clipped edges), outward volume checks flip winding for negative-determinant/south-up affines, and lazy `5 × 5`-probe `OverlayCullTree` balls (`veldmap-core::tile::overlay`, keeping `geo` acyclic) delegate visibility directly to `CullFrustum::admits`.
- **Why keep QOI codec helpers in `veldmap-raster`, off-UI-thread overlay I/O & native Windows RAM detection:** Keeping `encode_qoi_rgba8`/`decode_qoi_rgba8` (pure-Rust `qoi` crate, symmetric `16 MiB` bounds + sRGB/RGBA validation) inside `veldmap-raster` while `TileDiskCache` provides ABA-safe `get_encoded_entry`/`delete_entry` and cancellation-checked `put_encoded_batch_if_uncancelled` keeps `veldmap-storage` strictly codec-free. Running local file open/reload + `clear_source` on a dedicated overlay I/O `SingleThreadWorker` (`spawn_overlay_io_worker`) with versioned `(job_id, CancelToken)` pointwise scheduling prevents UI stalls and reload cache poisoning; native Windows `GlobalMemoryStatusEx` closes the last OS gap in `detect_memory_budget()`.

## 9. Pure-Rust CRS/WKT Expansion, Drone RGB Codecs, Subdivided Pass-2 Vectors & Workstation UX (Slice 3A-2)

- **Why hybrid in-house Krueger + `proj4rs`/`proj4wkt`/`crs-definitions` (and explicit `HelmertConvention`):** Retaining our fast exact 4th-order Krueger Transverse Mercator (`System`, extended with `origin_lat_deg` and `unit_to_metres` for International/US Survey Feet) and Web Mercator alongside pure-Rust `proj4rs` + `proj4wkt` + `crs-definitions` (`MIT`/`Apache-2.0`/`CC0-1.0`, zero C `libproj` deps) gives zero-overhead precision for common UTM/GK/MSK-xx rasters while covering arbitrary 2D EPSG codes and WKT1/WKT2 `.prj` sidecars. `Proj4Projection` rejects 3D geocentric CRSs (`is_geocent()`), falls back to a 3-parameter NAD27 shift with `DatumWarning::GridShiftRecommended` when optional `+nadgrids` files are absent, and inspects `ellipse_parameters()` so any unshifted non-WGS84 ellipsoid raises `UnshiftedForeignDatum`. Tagging `Datum::convention` explicitly as `HelmertConvention::CoordinateFrame` (EPSG:9607) vs. `PositionVector` (EPSG:9606 / WKT `TOWGS84`) prevents silent sign-flip bugs across datum sources, and wiring `Datum::to_wgs84`/`from_wgs84` into geographic `Frame` bindings shifts foreign-datum geographic rasters accurately.
- **Why direct TIFF `YCbCr -> RGBA8`, pure-Rust `webp`, `jpeg-decoder` over `zune-jpeg`, `BufRead` on `BufferedByteReader` & injected sidecars:** Converting raw upsampled YCbCr bytes directly to RGBA8 in `tiff/ycbcr.rs` using `YCbCrCoefficients` (tag 529, BT.601 default) and `ReferenceBlackWhite` (tag 532)—with per-IFD YCbCr tracking so YCbCr overviews on RGB GeoTIFFs work cleanly, now fed directly by raw-chunk `jpeg-decoder` (§10) and `SinglePassIfdReader` (§11)—avoids intermediate 3 B/px copies while pure-Rust `image-webp` (`0xC351`) covers WebP-compressed drone COGs with zero C codecs. `JpegSource` intentionally keeps `jpeg-decoder` (`default-features = false`, zero transitive deps) over `zune-jpeg` because `jpeg-decoder` supports DCT `scale()` downscaling and lossless/16-bit JPEGs, while enforcing full-resolution coefficient/sample peak accounting on progressive/lossless scans and upfront `set_max_decoding_buffer_size` limits. Implementing `std::io::BufRead` directly on `BufferedByteReader` lets `PngSource` stream non-interlaced rows directly into `Cascade` (enforcing upfront `png::Limits` and whole-frame budget caps on Adam7 PNGs), and loading `.tfw`/`.pgw`/`.jgw`/`.wld` + `.prj` sidecars at the opener seam keeps every raster decoder pure `ByteSource`.
- **Why exterior-pole stereographic projection, Conforming Midpoint Closure, per-cluster RTC & GPU-instanced points in Pass 2:** Projecting arbitrary spherical polygons via conformal stereographic projection from a point strictly outside the polygon handles polar, antimeridian, and `> 180°` (>hemisphere) polygons without hemisphere-splitting seams or pole blowups. Triangulating with pure-Rust `earcut`, subdividing edges longer than `max_chord_deg` via stereographic midpoints (`<= 0.5°`), running a read-only Conforming Midpoint Closure pass (`close_t_junctions`) to eliminate T-junction cracks along shared edges, and packing per-cluster `f64` RTC origins (`VectorCluster`) into shared layer vertex buffers preserves sub-millimetre precision at `2 m` zoom for drone-scale overlays alongside continental geometries. Rendering fills, strokes, and zoom-invariant GPU-instanced point markers (`vs_point`, `VertexStepMode::Instance`) in Pass 2 (`shaders/vector.wgsl`) reuses `DynamicUniformArena`'s `VectorUniform` dynamic slot (initially 32 bytes; expanded to 48 bytes in §11 for HiDPI ribbons and screen-space hatching) and `dot(in.normal, -in.world) <= 0.0` horizon clipping with zero CPU mesh rebuilds on zoom.
- **Why main-thread `rfd::AsyncFileDialog`, `dejavu` fallback & coalesced `session.json`:** Constructing `rfd::AsyncFileDialog::pick_files()` / `save_file()` on the UI thread with `.set_parent(frame)` (required for non-blocking AppKit sheets on macOS) and polling the returned `Send` future off-thread via `pollster::block_on` avoids UI freezes on all 3 OSes (`rfd 0.18` `xdg-portal` + `wayland` avoids `gtk3`/`glib` build deps on Linux, opening `libdbus-1` via `dlopen` when a portal is present and keeping drag-and-drop / `Path…` / CLI args as fallbacks). Layering `dejavu::sans::regular()` and `dejavu::sans_mono::regular()` behind `egui`'s defaults provides deterministic cross-OS non-Latin and GIS symbol coverage without checking font binaries into git. Persisting `session.json` via `write_json_durable_private()` with write-coalescing on `DiskCacheWorker` prevents UI `fsync` stalls and drains cleanly on shutdown.

## 10. Scientific & Categorical GPU Tracks, Direct Raw-Chunk GeoTIFFs, Byte-Shuffled `Lz4Raw` Cache & Live Stretch (Slice 3B)

- **Why direct raw-chunk GeoTIFF decoding over `ByteSource` (open-time `tiff` parsing subsequently replaced by `SinglePassIfdReader` in §11):** `tiff 0.11.3`'s `Decoder::read_chunk` unconditionally rejects `PhotometricInterpretation = 3` (`Palette`), truncates 4-band RGB+NIR (`ExtraSamples = 0`) tiles and corrupts their predictors, and fails on tiled `PlanarConfiguration = 2` NPOT bottom-edge chunks. Parsing and caching bounded per-IFD tables (`TileOffsets`/`StripOffsets`, `TileByteCounts`/`StripByteCounts`, `JPEGTables`, `ColorMap`, YCbCr params, and layout—bounded against `MemoryBudget` and `MAX_SINGLE_CHUNK_BYTES = 256 MiB`) once in `TiffSource::open` and decoding raw chunks lock-free over `ByteSource` using direct codec crates (`flate2`, `weezl`, `zstd`, `jpeg-decoder`, `image-webp`) resolved all three blockers, eliminated pooled stateful `Decoder` contention, validated JPEG/WebP decoded chunk dimensions upfront (using `jpeg-decoder`'s `ColorTransform::RGB` for 3-channel and inverted `CMYK` for 4-channel raw component output so `YCbCrParams` tags 529/532 apply cleanly without `ColorTransform::None`'s subsampling panic), kept `WhiteIsZero` inversion display-only after raw nodata checks, and exposed the cached IFD offset tables needed by Slice 4A remote COG range prefetching. *(Open-time `tiff::Decoder` was subsequently replaced in §11 by `SinglePassIfdReader`, moving `tiff` to `test-support`/dev-only.)*
- **Why three typed GPU tracks (`Rgba8UnormSrgb`, `R32Float` with NaN-skipping mips, `R8Uint`/`R16Uint` 1-mip) and `f32` display vs `f64` CPU readout:** Generalizing `RasterSource`, `DecodedChunk`, `Grid`, `Cascade`, and `driver::{direct, pass}` by `TileStreamSpec`/`PixelFormat` gates `bleed_alpha` strictly to `Rgba8UnormSrgb` so scientific and categorical samples are never smeared across nodata edges. `R32Float` tiles (`512 × 512`, `gutter = 0`, `~1.33 MiB` with full CPU NaN-skipping box-filter mipmaps including NPOT edge tiles) store raw numeric values with nodata/non-finite mapped to `f32::NAN`, while `R8Uint` / `R16Uint` categorical tiles (`gutter = 0`, 1 mip, nearest-neighbor downsampling) share `texture_2d<u32>` bindings without blending class IDs. Because IEEE-754 `f32` has a 24-bit mantissa (~7 decimal digits), GPU `R32Float` visualization quantizes 32-bit integers (`u32`/`i32`) and `f64` rasters beyond `2^24`; zoom-invariant CPU cursor readouts (`RasterSource::sample_band`) therefore read native precision in `f64` directly from the base IFD and evaluate canonical `raw * scale + offset` (matching GDAL `GDAL_METADATA` / STAC `raster:bands` / CF NetCDF).
- **Why manual 8-tap nodata-aware trilinear WGSL `textureLoad` + `bitcast<u32>` finiteness check + 256-entry `PaletteUniform`:** Sampling `R32Float` via hardware linear samplers requires the optional `FLOAT32_FILTERABLE` feature (absent on baseline WebGPU / `downlevel_defaults`) and smears `NaN` across 2×2 quads. Computing `dpdx`/`dpdy` mip LOD strictly in uniform control flow *before* horizon `discard` and evaluating two per-mip 4-tap `textureLoad(tile_tex, coord, mip)` bilinear taps (indexed against `textureDimensions(tile_tex, mip)` for NPOT tiles) eliminates nearest-mip zoom popping and minification shimmer without `FLOAT32_FILTERABLE`. Checking `(bitcast<u32>(v) & 0x7F800000u) != 0x7F800000u` strictly before any arithmetic prevents Metal fast-math optimizations from collapsing `NaN`/`±Inf` checks. Sharing one 4096-byte `PaletteUniform` (`256 × vec4<f32>` linear-light stops, well within baseline 16 KiB UBO limits) across both continuous scientific colormaps (linear and `10·log10` dB stretch) and categorical palettes (`SourceColorMap`, ESA Sentinel-2 `SCL`, deterministic hash) keeps both shaders within 4 bind groups.
- **Why byte-shuffled `lz4_flex` (`TileBlobEncoding::Lz4Raw`) disk caching:** Storing mip-0 `R32Float`/`R8Uint`/`R16Uint` tiles in `TileDiskCache` with a 24-byte self-describing `VML4` header (`magic`, `version`, `format`, `gutter`, `flags`, `width`, `height`, `valid_w`, `valid_h`, deriving `expected_bytes = width * height * bpp` before allocation), 4-byte / 2-byte lane shuffling (grouping float exponent/mantissa bytes for markedly higher LZ4 compression ratios), pure-Rust checked `lz4_flex` (MIT, zero `unsafe`), and an explicit pre-allocation bounds guard matches `Qoi` durability while regenerating `R32Float` mip chains on disk-cache hit.
- **Why `StreamKey`-keyed `UploadBatchReport`, hoisted multi-format `TileBindGroupCache` & multi-band VRAM budgeting:** Keying upload byte reports by `StreamKey` prevents `R32Float` (`~1.33 MiB`) and `R8Uint` (`256 KiB`) streams sharing one `SourceId` from mis-weighting each other's VRAM appetite. Hoisting `TileBindGroupCache` into `egui_wgpu::CallbackResources` (keyed by unique `GpuTile::tile_id` with `Weak<GpuTile>` sweeping across `Rgba8UnormSrgb`, `R32Float`, and `R8Uint`/`R16Uint` bind-group layouts) prepares clean texture sharing for future 2D orthographic / band-math views, while summing active stream weights across multi-stream `OverlayLayer`s, supporting opt-in `pin_root`, and exposing an optional `128–4096 MiB` GPU cache cap in Settings keeps multi-band EO working sets responsive without throttling into coarse overviews.

## 11. Cloud EO Streaming: Shared `NetContext`, HTTP Range COGs, Single-Pass IFD Reader, Pool Isolation & STAC Workbench (Slice 4A)

- **Why a shared `NetContext` with split identity/gzip clients, host-scoped cooldown clearing & split timeouts:** Consolidating the ≥2-thread `tokio::Runtime`, shared `HostLimiters`, bounded 64 KiB O(1) LRU `RangeBlockPool` (`32–256 MiB` host-RAM scaled), and upfront `PrefetchBudget` (`16–128 MiB`) into a single `Arc<NetContext>` owned by `SceneState` eliminates per-subsystem runtime proliferation while guaranteeing `LiveTilePipeline`, `HttpRangeSource`, and `StacClient` share per-host concurrency permits and rate-limit cooldowns. Keeping a non-decompressing `range_client` (`.gzip(false)`, explicit `Accept-Encoding: identity`, rejecting non-`identity` `Content-Encoding`) separate from a gzip-capable `api_client` (`.gzip(true)` for STAC JSON) prevents transparent HTTP decompression from corrupting byte-range offsets. Replacing `Client::timeout` with split `connect_timeout` + `read_timeout` avoids aborting legitimate large multi-megabyte Range responses mid-stream, and replacing global `clear_cooldowns()` with host-scoped `clear_cooldowns_for(hosts)` prevents retrying a basemap provider from wiping active `429`/`503` backoffs on unrelated COG/STAC hosts.
- **Why 1-RTT adaptive `0..256 KiB` head probes, strong-ETag-only `If-Match`, refcounted singleflight & zero-extra-RTT remote fingerprints:** Probing `bytes=0-262143` during `HttpRangeSource::open_async` (`416` `bytes */0`-safe on 0-byte objects) satisfies typical 128–300 KiB Sentinel-2 COG IFD and tile-offset headers in a single RTT, rejects `200 OK` full-body responses, validates `Content-Range` bounds, and enforces RFC 9110 strong-ETag-only `If-Match` (never sending weak `W/"..."`, treating `412 Precondition Failed` as non-retryable, and isolating pool entries without strong ETags via per-open `instance_id`). Refcounted `WaiterGuard` + `LeaderCleanupGuard` + pre-lock `tokio::pin!`/`Notified::enable()` singleflight guarantees N-way concurrent reads deduplicate with zero lost wakeups, cancelling Follower A never aborts an HTTP Range GET still awaited by Follower B, and callers never join an already-cancelled dying flight. Deriving remote `SourceFingerprint` (`veldmap-remote-v1`) from `(canonical_url_without_query, len, strong_etag)` (falling back to `blake3(head_64k)` when no strong ETag exists) costs zero extra HTTP round-trips and fits within `DiskTileKey`'s ASCII allowlist.
- **Why a pure-`ByteSource` `SinglePassIfdReader`, pinned coalesced prefetch & strict decode-pool isolation:** Replacing open-time `tiff::decoder::Decoder` with `SinglePassIfdReader` (256 KiB initial prefix aligned with `HttpRangeSource`'s head probe, Classic + BigTIFF, cycle detection, inline `NewSubfileType` mask skipping, pre-allocation `remote_ifd_limit_bytes` caps, and coalesced out-of-prefix tag reads) moves `tiff` entirely out of production dependencies (kept optional behind `test-support`/`dev-dependencies` for test fixture writers) and avoids double offset-table reads on remote COGs. Rejecting remote non-COG/stripped/PNG/JPEG sources with terminal `RasterError::RemotePassUnsupported` prevents accidental whole-file HTTP downloads. For remote pointwise tiles, `OverlayManager` plans required chunk + display-stretch byte ranges from cached IFD tables, coalesces and pins them asynchronously into a `PrefetchPin` on `NetContext`, and submits pure-CPU decode (capturing `PrefetchPin`) onto `TwoLaneDecodePool::fast()`. Remote opens (IFD parsing), remote `band_stats` (after async prefetch), and cache-only remote `CursorProber` queries run on a dedicated non-Tokio 2-worker `veldmap-remote-io` pool (ensuring `Handle::try_current().is_err()` holds if an oversized IFD overflows the pinned head, and preventing slow HTTP from starving local SSD decodes or `spawn_overlay_io_worker`). Offloading pointwise QOI/LZ4 encoding and batched SQLite commits onto a bounded `veldmap-tile-write` worker *after* GPU upload delivery—backed by `rusqlite`'s `"cache"` (`hashlink`) `prepare_cached` statements—keeps `fast_pool` strictly CPU-bound.
- **Why HiDPI screen-space ribbon outlines, diagonal hatch fills, retained vector AOIs & collapsible workbench shell:** WebGPU/Vulkan/DX12/Metal `LineList` primitives are locked to 1 physical pixel; instancing `GpuRibbonSegment` (48 B) in `shaders/vector.wgsl::vs_ribbon` with per-cluster `f64` RTC and `VectorUniform` (48 B, carrying `viewport_inv_size`, `stroke_half_width_px`, and `hatch_spacing_px`) yields crisp HiDPI-scaled outlines and screen-space diagonal hatch fills for STAC AOIs and hovered/selected scene footprints across all primary backends. Retaining parsed `GeoJsonFeatureCollection`s in `VectorManager`, promoting `"Basemap…"` to a 3-tab unified `Settings` modal (`Basemap`, `Cache & GPU`, `STAC / Cloud` with redacted secrets), and unifying the left panel into a collapsible multi-tab workbench (`Layers` | `Cloud / STAC`) keeps AOI drawing, GeoJSON/WKT export, built-in zero-auth sample scenes, and custom-auth STAC discovery ergonomic without temporary floating dialogs.

## 12. Multi-Asset Layers, Lazy Screen-Space Band Math Scratch Pool, MRT/Scissor Bounds & Per-Band Metadata (Slice 5A-1)

- **Why tagged `LayerKind` + cohesive `RasterAsset` and presentation-only `ui/` boundaries:** Refactoring `OverlayLayer` and `DrawOverlayLayer` into tagged `LayerKind::{SingleRaster, BandMath}` backed by cohesive `RasterAsset` units lets one multi-asset layer share `SourceId` refcounting, independent per-band LOD ladders, and async range-prefetch choreography across mixed-GSD bands (e.g., Sentinel-2 10 m/20 m/60 m) without duplicate raw layers or redundant camera flights, while staying naturally extensible to Slice 5B `DemMosaic` and `Timeline`. Projecting STAC state through read-only DTOs (`StacViewSnapshot`, `StacItemView`, `StacRecipeView`, `StacCogStreamSpec`) eliminates all direct `veldmap-net` and `veldmap-raster` imports from `ui/`.
- **Why lazy shared quiet-`NaN`-filled `R32Float` + `R8Uint` scratch passes, MRT & Pass-2 segmentation:** Evaluating band math directly inside a single warp-mesh shader breaks whenever bands differ in GSD, grid alignment (Landsat 15/30 m half-pixel shift), ancestor carrier level, or projection. Instead, `GlobeRenderer` segments Pass 2 around `BandMath` layers in painter's order while keeping Pass 1 as the sole consumer of `Depth32Float` (`TRANSIENT_ATTACHMENT` + `StoreOp::Discard`). `ScratchTargetPool` allocates `1..=4` `R32Float` targets (`blend: None`, never `TRANSIENT_ATTACHMENT`) plus an optional `R8Uint` mask target (cleared to `255` uncovered sentinel) lazily for the exact active band count and releases them when unused or viewport-collapsed. Initializing `R32Float` targets via `LoadOp::Clear(TRANSPARENT)` (avoiding TBDR DRAM readbacks) followed by a scissored fullscreen quiet-`NaN` uniform fill avoids WGSL compile-time NaN rejections in `naga` while preserving valid `0.0` reflectance. Co-grid bands sharing one exact `Frame` (verified beyond 4 corners to prevent cross-projection aliasing) and `cell_rect` list collapse into a `2..=4`-attachment MRT pass within `downlevel_defaults()` (`max_bind_groups == 4`, `max_color_attachments == 4`), while mixed-GSD bands merge their per-band NaN fill and draw into one pass per band; all scratch/composite passes clamp to conservative projected scissor bounds (full-target fallback on `clip.w <= near`, horizon straddle, or `> 4°` angular spans).
- **Why floored dynamic target VRAM accounting (`64 MiB` minimum tile-cache floor) in `veldmap-app`:** Dynamically subtracting `OffscreenTarget` (`8 B/px`) plus active scratch targets (up to `17 B/px`) from `GpuTileCache`'s effective cap inside `SceneState` keeps `veldmap-core::MemoryBudget` pure and static while preventing VRAM oversubscription, and the `64 MiB` floor prevents 5K HiDPI viewports on 4–8 GiB hosts from starving `GpuTileCache` to `0`.
- **Why `has_embedded_scale_offset`, per-band `ExternalBandMeta` & non-discarded `SCL` `< 32` classes:** Tracking `BandInfo::has_embedded_scale_offset` prevents external STAC `raster:bands` metadata from overriding a GeoTIFF that explicitly embedded `SCALE=1.0, OFFSET=0.0`, while per-band `(scale, offset, nodata, unit)` propagation (from `GDAL_METADATA` and STAC 1.0/1.1 `raster:bands`/`bands`) normalizes analytical bands into canonical `raw * scale + offset` reflectance without letting display nodata leak onto non-color bands. Opening `SCL` explicitly on `R8Uint` + `Sentinel2Scl` prevents `U8 BlackIsZero` assets from falling into Display RGBA, and keeping maskable classes `< 32` (notably ESA class `0` "No Data", often tagged `GDAL_NODATA = 0` on Sentinel-2 COGs) out of `SCL` nodata-discard guarantees both GPU masking and zoom-invariant CPU cursor readouts honor bit `0` while reserving `255` strictly as the uncovered-footprint sentinel.

## 13. Split-Screen Swipe, Sanitized Cloud/Band-Math Workspaces, MGRS Formatting & Cartographic PNG Export (Slice 5A-2)

- **Why `SwipeRenderState` lives outside `DrawSnapshot` with `LastRenderKey`-keyed physical `split_x`:** Passing normalized `split_t ∈ [0.0, 1.0]` and `(left_layer, right_layer)` outside `DrawSnapshot::build` keeps 120 Hz curtain dragging completely free of globe quadtree and overlay cull rebuilds while keying `LastRenderKey` on `Option<ResolvedSwipe>` (snapping physical `split_x` via shared `snap_split_x(split_t, width_px)` in both `GlobeRenderer::render_if_dirty` and the `egui::Painter` divider) so HiDPI fractional scaling never introduces 1-px drift and idle frames still trigger `0` GPU re-renders. Intersecting each `BandMath` layer's projected scissor rect with its swipe half-plane *before* scratch passes—and skipping both scratch and composite draws whenever the intersection is empty—guarantees `wgpu` never sees a zero-sized scissor rect, while explicitly restoring the full-viewport scissor before vectors and the procedural graticule keeps annotations unclipped across both halves and swipe-aware `CursorProber` filtering keeps pixel readouts WYSIWYG.
- **Why tagged v2 `session.json` / `.veldmap.json` with strict URL sanitization, imported-project origin fencing, same-root relative paths & slot-preserving `SessionRestoreTracker`:** Evolving `SavedLayerEntry` into a tagged schema (`local_raster` | `remote_cog` | `band_math`, with seamless untagged v1 deserialization fallback) lets heterogeneous local/remote `BandMath` recipes, remote COGs, swipe state, and vector `outline_only` flags round-trip in both auto-saved `session.json` and portable `.veldmap.json` project files (relativizing local paths to the project directory when on the same root/drive, including Windows verbatim prefixes, with absolute fallback). Stripping query strings, presigned `?sig=`/`?X-Amz-Signature=` tokens, `user:pass@` userinfo, and custom auth headers via `veldmap_net::sanitize_url_for_persistence` before writing—while downgrading imported `.veldmap.json` remote assets whose URLs do not match `settings.json`'s `custom_endpoint` origin to `StacCogAuthMode::None` before queuing or persisting—ensures shareable project files never leak or exfiltrate private credentials. Generalizing `SessionRestoreTracker` from `Path`-only keys to opaque restore tokens with preserved original painter's-order slots and workspace open epochs prevents stale prior-workspace opens from leaking into a loaded project and preserves offline/`401`/`403` remote COG, `BandMath`, and swipe entries cleanly across transient outages.
- **Why clean-frame `COPY_SRC` offscreen PNG readback + off-UI-thread CPU cartographic compositing via `veldmap-raster`:** Retaining `OffscreenTarget::color_texture` with `COPY_SRC` usage and triggering a `256 B`-aligned staging-buffer copy even when `is_clean_for == true` captures exact 1:1 sRGB globe frames without forcing redundant re-renders or blocking the UI thread (`map_async` + `Wakeup` polled non-blockingly via `PollType::Poll`). Compositing optional cartographic overlays (scale bar, rotated North arrow, scientific colorbar, acquisition date/caption) on a tracked background thread (joined deterministically on shutdown) and exposing a narrow `encode_png_rgba8` helper in `veldmap-raster` (plus `write_json_durable`/`read_json_limited` in `veldmap-storage`) keeps `veldmap-app` free of direct `png` and `serde_json` dependencies.
- **Why pure-Rust WGS84 → MGRS formatting in `veldmap-core`:** Keeping bidirectional WGS84 ↔ MGRS conversion pure-Rust inside `veldmap-core::geo::mgrs` (UTM bands `C`–`X` with Norway/Svalbard exceptions and 4-corner sub-cell overlap validation across grid convergence) avoids external GIS dependencies while keeping UI polar fallback copy (`"UPS N"`/`"UPS S"`) localized in `veldmap-app::ui::format`.

## 14. Pre-5B Capability Refactor, STAC Pagination/Profiles/Refresh & Dual-Epoch Temporal Diffing (Slice 5B-2a)

- **Why remove `OverlayLayer::primary_asset()`, tag `LayerSummaryKind`/`OverlayEdit`/`OverlayIoEvent`, and split `storage/layers.rs`:** Multi-epoch `Timeline` layers (and upcoming `0..N`-geocell `DemMosaic` layers) have `0` or many dynamically swappable assets, so panicking single-asset accessors break down. Replacing `primary_asset()` with capability accessors (`footprint()`, `source_pixel_size_m()`, `reload_target()`, `contribute_appetite()`, fallible `begin_reload`/`replace_reloaded`), embedding `LayerSummaryKind::{SingleRaster, BandMath, Timeline}` inside `LayerSummary`, nesting kind-specific `OverlayEdit` variants, generalizing `compute_patch_sets_scissor_rect` over a `WarpPatchSetRef` iterator, and splitting tagged persistence entries into `storage/layers.rs` keeps every layer kind uniform and type-safe across UI, rendering, cursor readouts, and persistence.
- **Why bounded STAC `links[rel="next"]` (`GET`/`POST` + `merge`), named STAC profiles & origin-fenced singleflight `401`/`403` `stac_item_href` refresh:** AWS Earth Search and modern STAC APIs paginate via `links[rel="next"]` carrying `method: "POST"`, `body`, and `merge: true` shallow merges; supporting both `GET` and `POST`+`merge` in `veldmap-net` (ignoring server-supplied `headers`, blocking HTTPS→HTTP downgrades and cross-origin auth leaks, and capping cumulative results at `500` items) unlocks multi-month AOI discovery without unbounded memory growth. Persisting named `StacProfile`s in `settings.json` (transparently migrating legacy flat fields) allows multi-catalog workflows. On restore `401`/`403` (matched structurally on `RangeOpenError::HttpStatus { code: 401 | 403, .. }` rather than error strings), `StacItemFlightCache` coalesces concurrent band refreshes on the same `stac_item_href` inside `veldmap-net` (keeping `veldmap-app` free of `tokio`), strictly fencing `item_auth` via `same_http_origin` (always `StacAuth::None` for `AuthTrust::ImportedProject`), verifying refreshed `assets[asset_key].href` against the persisted canonical URL (anti-hijack + HTTPS no-downgrade), stripping `Bearer`/`ApiKey` headers on presigned-query or off-origin URLs, and capping at `1` retry per restore.
- **Why unified offscreen dual-epoch scalar diff (`band_math_reduce.wgsl`) & 0%-bleed-through RGB cross-fade (`rgba_targets: 0..=2` on `@group(3)`):** Keeping all dual-epoch passes strictly within `downlevel_defaults()` (`max_color_attachments == 4`, `max_bind_groups == 4`) and `wgpu` texture-usage hazard rules over the union scissor rect `S_AB` (intersected with `ResolvedSwipe::scissor_for_layer` before scratch passes) lets arbitrary non-co-registered / mixed-GSD epochs compare cleanly on GPU:
  - *Scalar diff (`N <= 3` bands/epoch, including `N = 1` single-band passthrough and `N = 2` `dNDVI`/`dNBR`/`dNDWI`):* Epoch A rasterizes into `float_views[0..N]` + `mask`, then reduces via a dedicated non-overlapping `reduce_bind_groups[N - 1]` (binding ONLY `0..N` + `mask`, never `float_views[N]`) into `float_views[N]` (quiet-`NaN` for uncovered/masked pixels); Epoch B then rasterizes into `float_views[0..N]` + `mask` leaving `float_views[N]` intact, and composite evaluates `formula(B, mask_B) - scratch[diff_slot]` (preserving valid `0.0` diffs and independent Epoch A vs. Epoch B `SCL` masks).
  - *RGB cross-fade (0% basemap bleed-through):* Extending `ScratchTargetPool` with lazy `rgba_targets: 0..=2` `Rgba8UnormSrgb` targets (`+4 B/px` each, bound on `@group(3)` rather than `@group(0)` with a 1×1 `dummy_rgba_bind_group` bound during Epoch A bake to prevent simultaneous sampled+render-target hazards) lets `BandMathRgb` (`17 B/px` total) bake Epoch A into `rgba[0]` before rasterizing Epoch B, and lets `DirectDisplay` (`8 B/px`, zero `R32Float`) rasterize both epochs via a `blend: None` `display_scratch` pipeline before a single fullscreen `cross_fade_straight` composite (yielding 0% basemap bleed-through at `t = 0.5` where both epochs are valid and graceful single-valid fading along partial edges/cloud masks).
- **Why `Timeline` keeps a bounded LRU of open epochs (`<= 6`), protects adjacent prefetch via `wanted` sets, and avoids `purge_source` on scrub:** Grouping STAC AOI scenes by `mgrs_tile` (preferring L2A surface reflectance when levels mix) and scanning local folders off-thread via deterministic `ISO filename -> TIFF DateTime tag 306 -> lexicographic` ordering feeds `LayerKind::Timeline` (`pin_root = false`). Exposing adjacent filtered epochs (`active_idx ± 1`) in `OverlayLayer::streams()` at a coarse `4`-tile cap protects prefetched tiles from `PendingUploads::drain_and_upload` pruning and `OverlayManager::reconcile` cancellation while capping full appetite to actively rendered epochs; scrubbing cancels unneeded epoch-open `CancelToken`s immediately and leaves recently visited epochs warm in `GpuTileCache` rather than calling `purge_source`.
