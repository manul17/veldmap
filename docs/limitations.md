# Known Limitations

Explicit scope boundaries and current implementation limits of VeldMap. Entries are updated as slices close and capabilities land.

## Planetary Model & Platform (Committed v1 Scope)

- **Flat WGS84 ellipsoid (no 3D DEM mesh displacement in v1; nadir camera currently):** VeldMap v1 renders a smooth WGS84 ellipsoid (`height = 0.0`) with a nadir camera ([Design Decisions](decisions.md)). Terrain relief is delivered as quantitative GPU layers (hillshade, slope, aspect) in Slice 5B-1 rather than 3D vertex displacement, avoiding renderer-induced relief parallax and keeping uniform nadir GSD for orthophoto alignment, split-screen swipe, and temporal diffing. (Source-imagery relief distortion in non-orthorectified swaths is unaffected.)
- **Desktop native GPU backends only (Vulkan, DirectX 12, Metal):** VeldMap restricts `wgpu` to native primary desktop backends (`Backends::PRIMARY` minus `BROWSER_WEBGPU`), intentionally excluding OpenGL/GLES and browser WASM to keep Reverse-Z, `view_formats`, and `R32Float` behavior uniform across platforms. Systems without hardware Vulkan/DX12/Metal drivers require a software fallback such as Mesa Lavapipe or Microsoft WARP.

## Current Milestone Limits (Post-Slice 3A-2 / Milestone v0.2)

- **Local precision workstation complete (`v0.2`); scientific tracks & cloud COGs upcoming:** VeldMap `v0.2` supports offline/live HTTP basemaps (NASA GIBS, EOX Sentinel-2 Cloudless, Custom XYZ/TMS), local chunky (`PlanarConfiguration = 1`) 8/16-bit and `f32` GeoTIFF/BigTIFF/COG overlays (including ZSTD, JPEG/4:4:4 `YCbCr`, and pure-Rust `WebP` `0xC351`), local PNG/JPEG overlays with ESRI world files (`.tfw`/`.pgw`/`.jgw`/`.wld`) and `.prj` WKT1/WKT2 sidecars, read-only GeoJSON vector overlays, interactive Karney WGS84 distance/area measurement with RFC 7946 GeoJSON export, native OS file dialogs (`rfd` `xdg-portal`/`wayland`) with drag-and-drop/path fallback, and a Layer Manager with reordering, CRS overrides, metadata inspection, diagnostic banners, detail-limit captions, and loading shimmer. Scientific `R32Float`/`R8Uint`/`R16Uint` tracks (`Planar = 2`, `Palette`, 4-band RGBN) land in Slice 3B; STAC/COG HTTP Range streaming lands in Slice 4A; interactive band math & split-screen swipe land in Slice 5A (see [plan.md](../plan.md)).
- **Navigation & viewport controls:** Interactive controls cover primary-button drag orbit, right-drag / Shift+drag and `Q`/`E` manual heading rotation, Arrow/`WASD` keyboard pan, scroll/pinch/`+`/`-` zoom, Go-To DD/DMS/MGRS, camera bookmarks, live/offline/ellipsoid basemap switching & settings, graticule toggle, and `N`/compass Reset-North. Multi-viewport split panes / swipe curtains land in Slice 5A.
- **CRS, datum shifts, georeferencing & MGRS coverage:** `veldmap-core::geo` combines fast exact in-house projections (WGS84 UTM `326xx`/`327xx`, Pulkovo 1942 Gauss-Krüger `284xx`, MSK-61 `6336101..=6336103`, Web Mercator `3857`, and custom Transverse Mercator with arbitrary origin latitude and International/US Survey Feet) with pure-Rust `proj4rs` + `proj4wkt` + `crs-definitions` for broad EPSG catalogs, GeoTIFF user-defined GeoKeys (`3072 == 32767`), and WKT1/WKT2 `.prj` sidecars, with explicit `CoordinateFrame` (EPSG:9607) vs. `PositionVector` (EPSG:9606 / `TOWGS84`) Helmert conventions and visible UI warnings for unshifted or grid-shifted datums. Grid-based datum shifts (NADCON5/NTv2/OSTN15) are not bundled, so datasets on tectonic/grid-shifted datums may retain decimetre-to-metre residual offsets relative to WGS84/ITRF. Georeferencing covers affine projected/geographic rasters and regular rectangular tie lattices; scattered GCPs and Rational Polynomial Coefficients (RPCs, Tag `50844`) for unorthorectified imagery are outside v1 scope. MGRS parsing covers UTM bands `C`–`X` (including Norway `31V`/`32V` and Svalbard `31X`/`33X`/`35X`/`37X` bounds), excluding UPS polar zones (`A`, `B`, `Y`, `Z`).
- **UI fonts:** `eframe`'s built-in default fonts are paired with bundled DejaVu Sans and DejaVu Sans Mono fallbacks (`dejavu` crate), covering Latin, Extended Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Hebrew, Armenian, Georgian, and GIS symbols across all OSes. CJK ideographs are not bundled in the binary.
- **Settings & local session persisted; CDSE credentials & analytical workspace recipes upcoming:** Basemap settings persist durably to `settings.json`, and open layers, CRS overrides, GeoJSON vector layers, recent files, camera bookmarks, and last camera pose persist durably to `session.json` via `write_json_durable_private()` (`0600` on Unix). CDSE credentials (`ProjectDirs/.env`) land in Slice 4B, and band-math recipes / split-pane layouts extend session persistence in Slice 5A; GPU/logging overrides remain controlled via environment variables (`RUST_LOG`, `WGPU_BACKEND`, `WGPU_POWER_PREF`).
